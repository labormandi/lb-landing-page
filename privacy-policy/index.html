1. About Us and This Policy

Vishx Innovations (OPC) Private Limited is a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, in India. We develop and operate the Worker Chowk Application, a labour management platform for contractors, supervisors, and business owners managing daily wage workers.

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • All Users who register for or use the Worker Chowk Application.
  • All Worker Data entered into the Application by Users on behalf of their workers.
  • All visitors to any website or digital presence operated by the Company in connection with the App.

This Policy does not apply to third-party websites, services, or applications that may be linked to or integrated with the App. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third parties you interact with.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you register for an Account or use the App, you provide us with:

  • Full name, business name, and professional designation.
  • Mobile phone number and email address.
  • Business address and site location details.
  • Login credentials (username and password).
  • Payment and financial settlement records you enter into the App.

2.2 Worker Data Entered by You

As a User, you may enter personal data about the workers you manage. This may include:

  • Worker names, contact numbers, and home addresses.
  • Photographs and profile images of workers.
  • Government-issued identity documents, such as Aadhaar card numbers, PAN card details, or other identification documents.
  • Daily attendance records, work hours, and clock-in/clock-out timestamps.
  • Work assignments, contract details, and performance records.
  • Payment amounts, settlement histories, and wage-related information.

Important: You are responsible for ensuring that you have obtained valid and lawful consent from each worker before entering their personal data into the App. See Section 9 for your responsibilities as a data controller.

2.3 Location Data

With your explicit permission, the App may collect:

  • Real-time GPS coordinates of your device, used for site-based attendance verification.
  • Location history associated with specific attendance entries and work records.

Location access is only activated when you grant permission through your device settings. You may revoke this permission at any time, though doing so may limit certain App features.

2.4 Automatically Collected Technical Data

When you use the App, we automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Device identifiers such as device ID, model, and operating system version.
  • IP address and network connection type.
  • App version, session duration, and usage patterns.
  • Crash reports, error logs, and diagnostic data.
  • Time and date stamps of App interactions.

2.5 Communications Data

If you contact us for support or respond to our communications, we may collect and retain records of those interactions, including:

  • Emails, messages, or chat logs sent to or received from the Company.
  • Call records where applicable and permitted by law.
